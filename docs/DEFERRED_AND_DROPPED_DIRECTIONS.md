# Deferred and Dropped Directions

## Deferred

Data-dependent execution, empirical comparisons, figure preparation, result summarization, and external-source reconciliation are outside the current public staging scope. They are deferred rather than represented by placeholders, because this tree is intended to remain inspectable without access to private or downloaded material.

## Dropped from this public tree

Result-bearing documentation, figure specifications and assets, internal archive indexes, source metadata, access records, raw and derived outputs, and tests tied to research data or output locations have been excluded. Their exclusion is a release-boundary decision, not a statement about their quality or interpretation.

## Reconsideration

Reconsidering any deferred direction requires an owner decision on public data rights, an explicitly reviewed release boundary, and a methods-only documentation update before related material is introduced.
